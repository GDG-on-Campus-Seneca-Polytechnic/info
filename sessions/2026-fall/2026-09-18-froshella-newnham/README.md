## Description

Froshella 2026 ("Frosh Meets Coachella") is the SSF welcome fair for new students. GDG @ Seneca will have a table where students can meet the team, see what we built, try a short interactive demo, and join the club.

## Flow for each visitor

People walk up at random, so there's no fixed schedule. Each visit takes about 2 to 3 minutes:

| Step | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 10 s | "Want to spin for a sticker?" |
| Pitch | 30 s | Who we are, pointing at photos from past events and the timeline of upcoming events |
| Q&A | 30 to 60 s | Answer their questions, or ask what they study |
| Mini game | 60 s | Try the demo on the laptop or iPad |
| Join and spin | 20 s | Scan the QR code to join, then spin the prize wheel |

### Pitch

We're Google Developer Group @ Seneca. We're not only about Google. We're a student community for anyone into tech who wants to build things. You work on real projects with a team, learn Google tools, and get better at presenting and networking.

### Questions to expect

- I'm in first year and don't have much coding experience. Can I still build something? Yes. That's what Code Jams and team projects are for.
- What's coming up? The Info Session on 23 September, then a Code Jam and a hackathon.
