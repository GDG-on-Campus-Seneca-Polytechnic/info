# GDG on Campus brand kit

A reference for anyone building a deck, site, or tool for this chapter. Colors and
type here match Google's own current GDG on Campus organizer brand guide. Reuse
this instead of re-deriving colors from memory or from a generic "Google colors"
search — the GDG-specific values differ slightly from Google's general marketing
palette (notably the yellow).

## Typography

The official kit specifies **Google Sans** (regular/bold) with **Google Sans
Mono** as secondary. Google Sans was only released under the SIL Open Font
License in December 2025, and its current Google Fonts listing wasn't fully
verifiable at the time this was written — don't build a live event around it
sight unseen.

**Default to Roboto instead.** It's Google's long-established, unambiguously
OFL-licensed font, safe to self-host so nothing depends on venue wifi or a CDN
being reachable during an event. Revisit Google Sans once its Fonts.google.com
listing and license are confirmed live.

## Logo and usage rules

Google's Partner Marketing Hub (the current home of Google's trademark rules)
and multiple chapters' public brand pages agree on the practical rules that
matter for chapter-built tools:

- Don't recolor, distort, rotate, or apply effects to the official GDG lockup.
- Don't fold Google's marks (the four-dot motif, the "G", etc.) into your own
  product's logo — build your own mark instead, even if it uses the same
  palette. A tool's own logo should look like *its* brand, not a copy of
  Google's.
- Keep clear space around the official lockup when you do use it (roughly 20%
  of the logo's height).
- Never let a chapter-built tool imply Google made it or endorses it. Put a
  small, plain disclaimer somewhere visible: "GDG on Campus @ Seneca
  Polytechnic is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google LLC."
- The official downloadable kit (logo lockups, deck) is at
  `goo.gle/on-campus-brand-guide`. Chapter logo asset files typically live in a
  Google Drive folder that requires a Google sign-in — not something to assume
  is publicly link-shareable.
